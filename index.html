A simple local-first appointment diary.

Tap any 30-minute slot to add an appointment. Tap an appointment to edit or delete it. Use the compact Tasks panel to capture, edit and add optional notes to tasks, and Calendar to jump to another date; yellow dates contain appointments. Your data stays in this browser on this device.
